# Playback terms

**Original frame**

A captured picture from the source. A USB delivery can repeat an earlier picture.

**Generated frame**

An interpolated picture between two original frames, intended for the midpoint of their source times.

**Held frame**

An earlier picture reused in a display slot where interpolation is unsuitable, such as during a hard cut.

**Presentation gap**

The elapsed time between two frames reaching the display. A long gap can be visible even when average FPS stays high.
